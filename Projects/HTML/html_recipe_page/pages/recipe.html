Chicken Adobo

A classic Filipino dish that can be sweet, sour, or salty. Depending on your taste.

Prep Time:
10 mins
Cook Time:
50 mins
Marinate Time:
1 hr/60 mins
Total Time:
2 hrs/120 mins

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken cut into serving pieces
  • 3 pieces dried bay leaves
  • 60 ml soy sauce
  • 67 ml white vinegar
  • 10 cloves garlic crushed
  • 355 ml water
  • 60 ml cooking oil
  • 4 g sugar
  • 2 g whole peppercorn
  • Salt to taste

Equipment

  • 1 Mortar and Pestle for crushing the garlic to release more flavor
  • 1 Heavy-bottomed Pot holds heat evenly for steady simmering.

Instructions

  1. Crush the garlic using a mortar and pestle.
    10 cloves garlic
  2. Combine the chicken, soy sauce, and crushed garlic in a large bowl. Mix well and marinate for at least 1 hour. The longer the chicken marinates, the better the flavor.
    1 kg chicken, 60 ml soy sauce, 10 cloves garlic
  3. Remove the chicken pieces from the marinade using kitchen tongs. Scoop out the crushed garlic with a slotted spoon. Reserve the garlic and marinade separately.
    1 kg chicken, 60 ml soy sauce, 10 cloves garlic
  4. Heat a cooking pot and pour in 3 tablespoons of cooking oil.
    60 ml cooking oil
  5. Once the oil gets hot, pan-fry the marinated chicken for 2 minutes per side.
    1 kg chicken
  6. Remove the chicken from the pot and set it aside.
    1 kg chicken
  7. Pour the remaining cooking oil into the same pot.
    60 ml cooking oil
  8. Add the garlic from the marinade and sauté until it starts to brown.
    10 cloves garlic
  9. Return the chicken to the pot.
    1 kg chicke
  10. Add the remaining marinade, whole peppercorns, and dried bay leaves.
    3 pieces dried bay leaves, 60 ml soy sauce, 2 g whole peppercorn
  11. Pour in the water and let it boil.
    355 ml water
  12. Cover the pot and simmer for 30 minutes, or until the chicken becomes tender.
    1 kg chicken
  13. Pour in the vinegar. Let it boil for 1 minute without stirring. Stir, then continue simmering uncovered for 15 minutes.
    67 ml white vinegar
  14. Add the sugar and salt as needed. Stir, then turn the heat off.
    4 g sugar, Salt
  15. Transfer to a serving plate. Serve hot with rice. Share and enjoy!

Sinigang na Bangus

Delicious fish simmered in tamarind soup loaded with vegetables.

Prep Time:
10 mins
Cook Time:
30 mins
Marinate Time:
N/A
Total Time:
40 mins

Ingredients

  • 1.5 kg bangus (milkfish) cleaned and sliced
  • 44g Knorr Sinigang sa Sampaloc Mix (Original)
  • 2 bunch kangkong
  • 16 pieces sitaw (snake bean) cut into 2-inch pieces
  • 8 pieces okra
  • 3 pieces long green pepper (siling pansigang)
  • 2 medium tomato wedged
  • 2 medium yellow onion wedged
  • 50 ml fish sauce
  • 1.50 ml ground black pepper
  • 2.5 L water

Equipment

  • 1 Wide cooking pot wide enough to hold the bangus steaks in a single layer
  • 1 Slotted spoon for lifting the fish without breaking the steaks

Instructions

  1. Heat a cooking pot and pour in the water.
    2.5 L water
  2. Add the tomato and onion. Let the water boil.
    2 medium tomato, 2 medium yellow onion
  3. Add the bangus. Cover the pot and cook over medium heat for 8 to 12 minutes.
    1.5 kg bangus (milkfish)
  4. Add the Knorr Sinigang sa Sampaloc Mix. Stir and cook for 2 minutes.
    44g Knorr Sinigang sa Sampaloc Mix (Original)
  5. Put the long green peppers into the pot. Add the sitaw and okra. Stir, cover the pot, and cook for 5 to 7 minutes.
    3 pieces long green pepper (siling pansigang), 16 pieces sitaw (snake beans), 8 pieces okra
  6. Add the fish sauce and ground black pepper. Stir.
    50 ml fish sauce, 1.50 ml ground black pepper
  7. Put the kangkong leaves into the pot. Cover and turn off the heat. Let it stay for 5 minutes.
    2 bunch kangkong
  8. Transfer to a serving bowl. Serve.
  9. Share and enjoy!

Sarciadong Pechay at Itlog

Savory pechay cooked in tomato and egg seasoned with fish sauce.

Prep Time:
10 mins
Cook Time:
25 mins
Marinate Time:
N/A
Total Time:
35 mins

Ingredients

  • 15 pieces eggs 12 boiled, 3 beaten
  • 8 grams Maggi Magic Sarap
  • 4 bunches pechay
  • 3 pieces tomatoes medium ripe, chopped
  • 1 pieces onion medium, chopped
  • 6 cloves garlic chopped
  • 240 ml water
  • 5 ml fish sauce
  • 1 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 45 ml cooking oil

Equipment

  • 1 Wok or cooking pot A wide wok works best for even distribution of the beaten eggs

Instructions

  1. Boil 12 eggs in a pot of water until firm, about 10 to 12 minutes. Peel them, and then set aside.
    15 pieces eggs
  2. Beat the remaining 3 eggs in a bowl, and then set aside.
    15 pieces eggs
  3. Heat the oil in a wok or cooking pot over medium heat. Saute the onion for 1 minute.
    45 ml cooking oil, 1 pieces onion
  4. Add the garlic, and continue sauteing for another minute.
    6 cloves garlic
  5. Add the tomatoes. Cook until they soften, and then add the fish sauce and ground black pepper.
    3 pieces tomatoes, 5 ml fish sauce, 1 teaspoon ground black pepper
  6. Pour in the water, and let it boil. Continue cooking for 1 to 2 minutes.
    240 ml water
  7. Pour the beaten eggs into the pot in a slow stream. Let them set briefly, and then stir until well incorporated with the other ingredient
    15 pieces eggs
  8. Add the pechay, and cook for 1 to 2 minutes.
    4 bunches pechay
  9. Season with Maggi Magic Sarap. Add the boiled eggs, and cook for another minute.
    8 grams Maggi Magic Sarap, 15 pieces eggs
  10. Transfer to a serving bowl. Serve with rice.
