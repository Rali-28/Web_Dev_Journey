Chicken Adobo

A classic Filipino dish that can be sweet, sour, or salty. Depending on your taste.

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken cut into serving pieces
  • 3 pieces dried bay leaves
  • 60 ml soy sauce
  • 67 ml white vinegar
  • 10 cloves garlic crushed
  • 355 ml water
  • 60 ml cooking oil
  • 4 g sugar
  • 2 g whole peppercorn
  • Salt to taste

Equipment

  • 1 Mortar and Pestle for crushing the garlic to release more flavor
  • 1 Heavy-bottomed Pot holds heat evenly for steady simmering.

Instructions

  1. Crush the garlic using a mortar and pestle.
    10 cloves garlic
  2. Combine the chicken, soy sauce, and crushed garlic in a large bowl. Mix well and marinate for at least 1 hour. The longer the chicken marinates, the better the flavor.
    1 kg chicken, 60 ml soy sauce, 10 cloves garlic
  3. Remove the chicken pieces from the marinade using kitchen tongs. Scoop out the crushed garlic with a slotted spoon. Reserve the garlic and marinade separately.
    1 kg chicken, 60 ml soy sauce, 10 cloves garlic
  4. Heat a cooking pot and pour in 3 tablespoons of cooking oil.
    60 ml cooking oil
  5. Once the oil gets hot, pan-fry the marinated chicken for 2 minutes per side.
    1 kg chicken
  6. Remove the chicken from the pot and set it aside.
    1 kg chicken
  7. Pour the remaining cooking oil into the same pot.
    60 ml cooking oil
  8. Add the garlic from the marinade and sauté until it starts to brown.
    10 cloves garlic
  9. Return the chicken to the pot.
    1 kg chicke
  10. Add the remaining marinade, whole peppercorns, and dried bay leaves.
    3 pieces dried bay leaves, 60 ml soy sauce, 2 g whole peppercorn
  11. Pour in the water and let it boil.
    355 ml water
  12. Cover the pot and simmer for 30 minutes, or until the chicken becomes tender.
    1 kg chicken
  13. Pour in the vinegar. Let it boil for 1 minute without stirring. Stir, then continue simmering uncovered for 15 minutes.
    67 ml white vinegar
  14. Add the sugar and salt as needed. Stir, then turn the heat off.
    4 g sugar, Salt
  15. Transfer to a serving plate. Serve hot with rice. Share and enjoy!
